DA-tuts — marimo pilot (T1–T4)

Self-contained, no-install, editable and re-runnable notebooks running entirely in your browser via WebAssembly — no server, no login.

After opening a notebook, cells don't auto-run (a marimo WASM quirk) — press Cmd/Ctrl+K, type "run all", and hit Enter once to execute everything.

  • T1 – DA & Introduction
  • T2 – Gaussian distribution
  • T3 – Bayesian inference
  • T4 – Time series filtering

Pilot only: T5–T9 aren't converted yet. Cross-notebook navigation and this landing page are hand-built for the pilot, not a marimo feature. Cells meant to be hidden (answer reveals, widget setup) show as a translucent, read-only code preview rather than being fully invisible — a known, currently-open marimo limitation in editable views (upstream issue marimo#5244).
